Workshop on causal inference · 2024

In collaboration with the doctoral school 393, the CIPHOD and PEPITES teams of IPLESP are co-organizing a workshop on causal inference, which will be held on June 5, 6, and 7 in room 203 at Sorbonne University - Faculty of Health – Saint-Antoine Hospital. The number of participants is limited to 20 doctoral students from ED393 and 20 members of IPLESP.

This workshop aims to introduce the basic concepts of causal inference.

Schedule

June 3 9h-10h Tracing History
June 3 10h15-11h Tools to ascend the ladder of causation (Part 1)
June 3 11h-12h Tools to ascend the ladder of causation (Part 2)
June 3 14h-15h Tools to ascend the ladder of causation (Part 3)
June 3 16h-18h Poster session
June 4 9h-12h Causal estimation
June 4 14h-18h On hands application (you can optionally come with your data)
June 5 9h-16h On hands application (you can optionally come with your data)
